## Feature Deep Dives

### 🎯 ABC/XYZ Analysis (Unique)
Most SMB platforms don't offer this enterprise-grade feature:
- **A-items:** High value (70% of consumption value, 10% of SKUs)
- **B-items:** Medium value (20% of value, 20% of SKUs)
- **C-items:** Low value (10% of value, 70% of SKUs)
- **X-items:** Stable demand
- **Y-items:** Variable demand
- **Z-items:** Unpredictable demand

**Benefit:** Focus management effort where it matters most

### 🤖 AI-Powered Demand Forecasting
- Multiple forecasting methods (moving average, exponential smoothing, ML-based)
- Seasonal adjustment
- Promotion impact analysis
- Accuracy tracking (MAE, MAPE)

**Benefit:** Reduce stockouts by 30-50%, reduce carrying costs by 20-30%

### 📦 Landed Cost Tracking
Track all costs to get products to your warehouse:
- Freight charges
- Import duties
- Insurance
- Customs brokerage
- Handling fees

**Benefit:** True profit margin calculation, accurate product costing

### 🔄 Consignment Inventory
- Track supplier-owned stock
- Automatic commission calculations
- Pay-on-sale workflows
- Return unsold stock

**Benefit:** Expand product offerings without capital investment

### 🏭 Complete Manufacturing
- Multi-level BOMs
- Work order management
- Component allocation
- Production tracking
- Yield tracking

**Benefit:** Full production visibility from raw materials to finished goods
